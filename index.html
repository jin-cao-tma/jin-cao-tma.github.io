About· 01 ·

I'm Jin Cao, a Computer Science PhD student at UT Austin, advised by Prof. Qixing Huang and Prof. Georgios Pavlakos. During my undergraduate, I was fortunate to collaborate with Prof. Jiajun Wu and Koven Yu from Stanford, Prof. Sida Peng and Prof. Xiaowei Zhou from ZJU3DV, Zhejiang University, Dr. Kaipeng Zhang from Alaya Lab, Shanda AI, and Prof. Xiangyong Cao and Prof. Deyu Meng from Xi'an Jiaotong University.

My research interest is to build virtual worlds that digitalize the physical world and enable downstream applications such as entertainment and robotics. Toward this goal, I focus on learning world representations from multi-modal data and building generative models on top of them. Recently, I have been interested in long-context generative world modeling, physically-grounded generation, and controllable generation in support of this goal.

I'm actively looking for long-term industry collaborations and internships. If you have relevant opportunities or resources, I'd love to connect — xjincaox@gmail.com.
